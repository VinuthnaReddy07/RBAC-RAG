HR LEAVE POLICY – 2026

Annual Leave:

* Full-time employees receive 24 paid leave days annually.

Sick Leave:

* Employees receive 12 sick leave days annually.

Maternity Leave:

* 26 weeks paid leave.

Paternity Leave:

* 4 weeks paid leave.

Emergency Leave:

* Requires manager approval.

Leave Carry Forward:

* Maximum 10 unused leave days may be carried forward.
